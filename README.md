## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📡 [5G Guardian](https://github.com/manikandan151113/5G-Guardian) — Android Network Monitoring Utility (FOSS)
* **Technologies:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material Design 3, Android Telephony APIs, Foreground Services, Robolectric
* **License:** GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)
* **Overview:** A privacy-first, offline-first Android application that continuously monitors mobile network conditions in real time and immediately alerts users when their cellular connection drops from high-speed **5G** down to **4G, 3G, or 2G**.
* **Key Technical Implementations:**
  * **Persistent Foreground Service:** Engineered an ongoing foreground service with notification channel priority to ensure uninterrupted monitoring across all Android versions.
  * **Multi-Listener Telephony Pipeline:** Blended `TelephonyCallback.DisplayInfoListener` and `ServiceStateListener` with scheduled queries to maintain precise cellular state detection even during device sleep.
  * **Reliable Audio Subsystem:** Integrated `PowerManager.PARTIAL_WAKE_LOCK` with `MediaPlayer.setWakeMode` to deliver audible fallback alarms during deep sleep states.
  * **Call-State Intelligence:** Integrated `PhoneStateListener` to automatically suppress audible sirens during incoming or active phone calls, followed by an automated 30-second post-call cooldown.
  * **Automated Test Suite:** Built and verified 37 automated Robolectric unit tests covering state transitions, alarm decision trees, call muting logic, and simulation modes.
  * **100% FOSS & F-Droid Ready:** Zero network requests, zero trackers, zero proprietary SDKs, and completely offline operation.
